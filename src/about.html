About the Club

Carlisle Soccer Club has been the home of youth soccer in Carlisle, Iowa for players of all ages and abilities.

Who we are

Carlisle Soccer Club (CSC) is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to providing quality youth soccer opportunities for the Carlisle community. Every coach, referee coordinator, and board member is a volunteer — most of them parents of players in the club.

Our mission

We exist to teach the game, develop players and people, and make soccer accessible and affordable for every family in our community. We believe kids learn best when they are playing, and that a positive sideline is just as important as a good practice plan.

What we offer

  • Recreational soccer — fall and spring seasons with an emphasis on participation, fundamentals, and fun.
  • Select soccer — competitive teams for players seeking a higher level of training and travel competition.
  • Coach and referee development — training and support for new volunteers.

Our values

  • Everyone plays. Recreational players get meaningful playing time every match.
  • Respect the game. Players, parents, coaches, and referees are held to the same standard of conduct.
  • Safety first. Weather, field, and concussion protocols are followed without exception.
  • Community over trophies. Wins are nice; a kid who wants to come back next season matters more.
