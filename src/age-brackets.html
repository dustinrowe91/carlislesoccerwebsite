Age Brackets

Youth soccer age groups are based on birth year, not school grade. Here's how to find your player's group.

How age groups work

The 2026–27 soccer year runs from August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2027. Age groups use an August 1 cutoff, the same as the school year, so a player's age group depends on their full birth date, not just the year they were born.

Recreational teams combine two age groups into one division, so most kids play with their own grade and the grade next to it.

Quick check: born January through July? Subtract the birth year from 2027. Born August through December? Subtract it from 2026. Example: born March 2017 → U10; born September 2017 → U9.

Playing up

Players may request to play in an older age group. Requests are reviewed by the recreational or select director based on the player's ability, safety, and roster space. Playing down is not permitted.
