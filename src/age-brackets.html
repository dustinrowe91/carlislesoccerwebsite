Age Brackets

Youth soccer age groups are based on birth year, not school grade. Here's how to find your player's group.

How age groups work

US Youth Soccer and Iowa Soccer use a birth-year system. The soccer year runs from August 1 through July 31, so a player's age group is determined by the calendar year in which they were born — not their grade in school.

Quick formula: Subtract your player's birth year from the year the season ends (for a fall 2026 season, that's 2027). The result is their age group. Example: born in 2017 → 2027 − 2017 = U10.

Combined age groups

Because Carlisle is a smaller club, some recreational divisions combine two birth years (for example U9/U10) to field complete teams. The club aims to keep players with their peers whenever roster numbers allow.

Playing up

Players may request to play in an older age group. Requests are reviewed by the recreational or select director based on the player's ability, safety, and roster space. Playing down is not permitted.
