Become a Referee

Get paid, stay close to the game, and help your club. Players 13 and older are welcome.

Why referee?

  • Get paid per match — one of the best first jobs around.
  • Flexible schedule; you choose the matches you take.
  • Learn the game from the inside and become a better player.
  • Our club always needs officials, especially for younger age groups.

Requirements

  • Minimum age of 13 (check the current Iowa Referee Committee requirement).
  • Complete the online and in-person grassroots referee course.
  • Background check and SafeSport training for officials 18 and older.
  • Your own referee uniform and equipment.

How to get certified

  1. Visit the Iowa Referee Committee page and find an upcoming grassroots course.
  2. Register and complete the online modules, including the Laws of the Game.
  3. Attend the in-person field session.
  4. Pass the certification assessment and receive your referee badge.
  5. Let our referee coordinator know you're certified so you can get on the assignment list.

Gear you'll need

A basic kit includes a yellow jersey, black shorts and socks, whistle, watch, cards, and a notebook. Assistant referees also need flags.

New referee support

Nobody works their first match alone. New officials are paired with experienced referees and mentors, and start on small-sided games before moving up to older age groups.
