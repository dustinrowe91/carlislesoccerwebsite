Becoming a coach

You do not need to have played soccer to coach recreational teams. What you need is time, patience, and a willingness to learn alongside your players. The club provides practice plans and mentoring.

  1. Volunteer during registration or contact the recreational director.
  2. Complete the required background check through Iowa Soccer.
  3. Complete SafeSport and concussion awareness training.
  4. Pick up your team's equipment bag and roster before the first practice.

Match rules

  • Every recreational player receives substantial and roughly equal playing time.
  • Substitutions are made at stoppages as allowed by the league format.
  • Rosters must match what's in PlayMetrics — no unregistered players.
  • Coaches remain in the technical area and coach positively.
  • Home teams provide the game ball, corner flags, and nets.
  • Score-running rules apply in younger divisions per Rec Central guidance.

Rec Central rules

Carlisle recreational teams play in the Rec Central league. The league rulebook governs match play, roster rules, and discipline. Coaches should read it before the first match each season.

The league rulebook PDF should be linked here once uploaded to the new site.

Reporting scores

  1. Home coach reports the final score after every match, ideally the same day.
  2. Report cards, injuries, or incidents to the recreational director within 24 hours.
  3. Notify the referee coordinator if a match went unofficiated.

Running a good practice

  • Keep everyone moving — avoid lines and long instructions.
  • Every drill should involve a ball for every player when possible.
  • End every session with a scrimmage; it's why they came.
  • Praise effort and decisions, not just goals.

Weather & safety

Stop activity immediately for lightning and clear the field for 30 minutes after the last strike. Suspect a concussion? The player sits and cannot return without clearance. See game information for the full weather policy.
