Code of Conduct

One standard for everyone: play hard, play fair, respect the game and each other.

Players will

  • Play by the Laws of the Game and accept the referee's decisions.
  • Treat teammates, opponents, coaches, and officials with respect.
  • Give full effort at practices and matches and support their teammates.
  • Shake hands after every match, win or lose.
  • Never use abusive, profane, or discriminatory language.

Parents and spectators will

  • Cheer positively for all players — both teams.
  • Leave coaching to the coach and officiating to the referee.
  • Remain on the spectator side of the field, away from team benches.
  • Never confront a referee, coach, or player before, during, or after a match.
  • Model good behavior; kids copy what they see on the sideline.

Coaches will

  • Put player safety and development ahead of winning.
  • Give every recreational player meaningful playing time.
  • Set the standard for respect toward officials and opponents.
  • Complete all required background checks and training.
  • Communicate clearly and promptly with families.

Zero tolerance for referee abuse

Many of our officials are teenagers. Any harassment of a referee — by a coach, player, or spectator — results in removal from the facility and possible suspension from future matches.

Facility rules

  • All fields are tobacco, vape, and alcohol free.
  • No pets at fields during matches and practices.
  • Park in designated areas only.
  • Clean up your area before you leave.

Consequences

  1. Warning — the coach or a board member addresses the behavior.
  2. Removal — removal from the facility for the remainder of the match.
  3. Suspension — suspension from one or more matches, decided by the board.
  4. Dismissal — removal from the club without refund for severe or repeated violations.

To report a code of conduct concern, contact the club. Reports are handled confidentially by the board.
