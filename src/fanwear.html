Fan Wear

Rep the Pride. Club apparel for players, parents, and grandparents on the sideline.

Shop the club store

Our fan wear store opens periodically each season with hoodies, tees, hats, blankets, and chair bags in club colors. Orders are produced in batches and distributed through team coaches.

Club to confirm: link the current online spirit wear store and the open/close dates for the ordering window.

Ask about the current store

How ordering works

  1. The store opens for a limited window each season — watch Facebook and PlayMetrics.
  2. Place your order online and pay at checkout.
  3. Items are produced after the window closes, typically 2–3 weeks.
  4. Orders are handed out at the fields or through team coaches.

Where the money goes

Proceeds from fan wear support the club directly — equipment, field maintenance, referee costs, and the financial assistance fund.

Sponsors

Local business sponsorship keeps registration fees low. If you'd like to sponsor a team or the club, get in touch.

Fan wear is not the same as the required match uniform. See uniforms for what players must wear on game day.
