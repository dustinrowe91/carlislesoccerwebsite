Financial Assistance

Cost should never be the reason a kid doesn't play. Here's how we help.

What's available

Carlisle Soccer Club offers need-based assistance that can cover part or all of a player's registration fee. Assistance is funded by the club, sponsors, and donations from club families.

Who qualifies

  • Families experiencing financial hardship
  • Households qualifying for free or reduced-price school meals
  • Any family for whom registration fees are a barrier — just ask

How to apply

  1. Begin registration in PlayMetrics as normal.
  2. Select the financial assistance option at checkout, or contact the club treasurer before paying.
  3. Provide a brief description of your need. Documentation may be requested but is kept confidential.
  4. You'll receive a decision before rosters are finalized.

Confidentiality

Assistance requests are reviewed only by the treasurer and club president. Coaches, teammates, and other families are never told which players receive assistance.

Other ways to reduce costs

  • Payment plans spread over the season
  • Used uniform and cleat exchange between families
  • Volunteering as a coach or referee — see volunteer opportunities

Want to help? Donations to the assistance fund go directly to keeping local kids on the field. Get in touch to contribute or sponsor.
