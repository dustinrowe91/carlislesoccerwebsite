Game day checklist

  • Arrive 20–30 minutes before kickoff for warm-ups
  • Full uniform, shin guards under socks, and appropriate cleats
  • Water bottle and, for younger age groups, sunscreen
  • No jewelry, watches, or hard hair accessories — referees will check
  • Spectators sit on the opposite side of the field from team benches

Weather policy

Matches are cancelled or suspended for lightning, severe storms, or unsafe field conditions. If lightning is observed, play stops immediately and everyone leaves the field for at least 30 minutes after the last strike.

Cancellations are communicated through PlayMetrics and our Facebook page as early as possible. When in doubt, check before you drive.

Reporting scores

Home team coaches are responsible for reporting scores after each match, per Rec Central rules. Scores should be submitted the same day when possible.

  1. Log in to the league score reporting system.
  2. Find your match and enter the final score.
  3. Report any red cards or incidents to the recreational director.
