Recreational and select soccer

Our recreational program is built around participation, skill development, and sportsmanship. Teams practice once or twice a week and play a weekend match schedule within the Rec Central league.

For players looking for a higher level of competition, our select program offers year-round training and travel play through Iowa Soccer sanctioned competition.
