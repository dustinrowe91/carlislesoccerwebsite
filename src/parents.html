Frequently asked questions

How do I find out my player's team and schedule?

Team assignments and schedules are published in PlayMetrics before the season starts, and your coach will reach out directly.

What if my player wants to be on a team with a friend?

Requests can be noted during registration. We try to honor them in recreational divisions but cannot guarantee them once rosters are balanced.

How much does it cost?

Fees vary by program and season and are shown in PlayMetrics at registration. See registration info.

What if we can't afford registration?

Apply for financial assistance. Requests are confidential.

Do games happen in bad weather?

Light rain, yes. Lightning or unsafe fields, no. Cancellations are posted to PlayMetrics and Facebook. See the weather policy.

Can I help even if I don't know soccer?

Absolutely. Team parents, field setup, and board roles need no soccer experience. See volunteer opportunities.

Who do I contact with a question?

Start with your coach for team matters, or contact the club for anything else.
