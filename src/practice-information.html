Practice Information

How often teams train, what to bring, and what parents can expect.

How practices are scheduled

Coaches set their own practice days and times based on field availability and their team's schedule. You'll receive the schedule from your coach shortly after teams are announced, and it will also appear in your PlayMetrics calendar.

What to bring

  • Soccer ball, correctly sized for the age group
  • Shin guards and cleats (tennis shoes are acceptable for the youngest players)
  • Full water bottle — hydration matters, especially in August
  • Weather-appropriate layers; practices run in light rain and cold

Attendance

Let your coach know in advance when your player will miss a practice. Recreational soccer is meant to fit around family life, but consistent attendance helps teams learn together and keeps everyone safe.

Parent expectations

  • Drop off and pick up on time — coaches are volunteers with families too.
  • Stay for the session if your player is in the youngest age groups.
  • Let the coach coach; save questions for before or after the session.
  • All practice sites are tobacco and smoke free.

Weather cancellations

Practices are cancelled for lightning, severe weather, or unsafe field conditions. Your coach will communicate cancellations directly, usually through PlayMetrics.
