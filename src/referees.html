Before the match

  • Arrive at least 20 minutes early and inspect the field, nets, and corner flags.
  • Check player equipment: shin guards under socks, no jewelry, safe footwear.
  • Confirm rosters and coaches' credentials where required.
  • Introduce yourself to both coaches and set expectations for sideline behavior.

After the match

  • Confirm the final score with both coaches.
  • Report any cards, ejections, or incidents to the referee coordinator the same day.
  • Submit your game report if required by the league.

Uniforms and gear

Referees supply their own kit: jersey, shorts, socks, whistle, watch, cards, and a flag set for assistant duties.
