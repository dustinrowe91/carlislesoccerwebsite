Refund Policy

How refunds are handled when a player is unable to complete a season.

General policy

Registration fees pay for league registration, referees, uniforms, equipment, and field costs — most of which the club commits to well before the season starts. For that reason, refunds are limited once rosters are formed.

Refund windows

When you requestRefund
Before registration closesFull refund less any non-refundable processing fees
After registration closes, before teams are formedPartial refund less uniform and league costs
After teams are formed or the season beginsNo refund
Season-ending injury before the season beginsReviewed case by case with documentation

Exact amounts and cutoff dates are set each season by the board. Confirm the current season's dates before requesting.

How to request a refund

  1. Contact the club with the player's name, program, and reason for the request.
  2. The registrar and treasurer review the request against the policy above.
  3. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, typically within 2–4 weeks.

Cancelled seasons

If the club cancels a season entirely, families receive a credit or refund of unspent fees. Costs already committed on the club's behalf (uniforms, league registration) may not be refundable.

Financial hardship? Before withdrawing, please look at our financial assistance options. We would rather have your player on the field.
