Registration Info

Everything you need to get your player signed up for the upcoming season.

Register online

All Carlisle Soccer Club registration is handled through PlayMetrics. Create a family account once and use it for every season and every player in your household.

Register for Fall 2026

What you'll need

  • Player's full legal name and date of birth
  • Parent/guardian contact information
  • Uniform sizing (see uniforms)
  • Payment method — or a financial assistance request
  • Acknowledgement of the code of conduct

How to sign up

  1. Create or log in to your PlayMetrics family account.
  2. Select the current season's program for your player's age bracket.
  3. Complete the player profile and required waivers.
  4. Choose uniform sizes and add-on items if prompted.
  5. Pay the registration fee or submit an assistance request.
  6. Watch for a team assignment email from your coach before the season starts.
