Select Registration

Competitive, travel-based soccer for players ready for a higher level of training and match play.

Is select right for my player?

Select soccer asks for a bigger commitment than recreational: more training sessions per week, travel to matches and tournaments, and a season that extends beyond the traditional fall and spring windows. Players are placed on teams through tryouts rather than by neighborhood or school.

What's included

  • Multiple training sessions per week with a designated team coach
  • League play sanctioned through Iowa Soccer
  • Tournament entries as determined by each team
  • Select uniform kit (purchased separately from rec uniforms)

Tryouts

Tryouts are typically held in late spring for the following competitive year. Dates, locations, and age groups are announced on our Facebook page and in PlayMetrics.

  1. Register for tryouts in PlayMetrics.
  2. Attend all tryout sessions for your player's age group if possible.
  3. Offers are extended after evaluations are complete.
  4. Accept your offer and complete select registration and payment.

Costs

Select fees cover coaching, league and tournament entries, referees, and field use. Fees vary by age group and are published with each tryout announcement. Financial assistance is available.

Not ready for select? That's completely fine. Our recreational program is a great place to keep developing, and players move between programs every year.
