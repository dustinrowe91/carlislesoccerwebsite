Recreational uniform

Recreational players receive a club jersey as part of registration. Families supply the rest of the kit.

Select uniform

Select players purchase a full kit (home and away jerseys, shorts, socks, and training gear) through the club's approved supplier. Kits are typically used for multiple seasons. Ordering details are provided after team placement.

Sizing tips

  • Order jerseys with a little room to grow — kids grow fast between seasons.
  • Cleats should fit snugly with about a thumb's width at the toe.
  • Slip-in shin guards work well for older players; strap-on guards suit younger ones.

Not allowed on the field

  • Jewelry of any kind, including earrings and smart watches
  • Hard hair accessories, casts, or splints without approved padding
  • Metal-studded or toe cleats (baseball cleats)

Outgrown gear? We keep a cleat and shin guard exchange going between families. Contact us to donate or request items.
